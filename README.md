Entry for the make.fun competition [Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922).

## Why this line

Candidates: (A) Chief Regret Officer for Autonomous Zamboni Fleets, (B) the chosen grief-counselor line, (C) Senior Vice President of Apologising to the Group Chat. B won against the judge brief: it sounds like a real, hireable job with one hilariously specific twist, you can picture the person doing it (printers do smell fear), and it reads instantly at 53 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) validates the fixed entry line against the competition gate and writes it to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), then reads the file back to verify the bytes.
